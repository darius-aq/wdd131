Peru is the 3rd largest country in South America and is located on the western coast of the continent next the Pacific Ocean. It's one of the world's top megadiverse nations, having a massive percentage of the planet's diverse ecosystems across its coast, high Andes, and vast Amazon. The country is best known for Machu Picchu, its ancient Incan history and its world-renowned gastronomy.
